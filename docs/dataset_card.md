# Dataset Card

## Dataset Purpose

This dataset supports offline reproduction of vehicle-accident data classification, task-triggered proof-level estimation, authentication-decision, and policy-ablation experiments.

## Data Source

The base accident records are derived from UK Department for Transport STATS19 2024 road-safety open data. Raw STATS19 files are not included in this release package.

## Construction

The project samples and processes STATS19-derived accident records, then adds semi-synthetic experiment fields describing data-content sensitivity, access/request context, output scope, and fixed-rule policy decisions.

## Field Types

- STATS19-derived accident fields: collision severity, counts, time, road/weather context, and related aggregated accident features.
- `content_*`: semi-synthetic content-sensitivity fields.
- `access_*`: semi-synthetic request/task-context fields.
- `output_scope`: output-scope category used by the policy experiment.
- Decision outputs: refined base level, PTFa level, required authentication level, and proof mode.
- Rule-reference labels: labels generated by rules/policies in the experiment pipeline.

## Splits

- Full: 5,500 rows.
- Tune: 3,850 rows.
- Test: 1,650 rows.
- Final classified test: 1,650 rows.

The fixed tune/test split should be preserved for reproducibility.

## Label Meaning

`base_manual_label`, `access_ptf_reference_level`, and `required_auth_manual_label` are rule-reference or policy-reference labels. They are not independent human annotations and should not be presented as external ground truth.

## Suitable Uses

- Reproducing the offline data-classification and authentication-decision experiments.
- Auditing fixed-rule policy outputs.
- Studying policy-relative ablations under frozen rules.
- Teaching or benchmarking reproducibility workflows for semi-synthetic policy datasets.

## Unsuitable Uses

- Production data-capsule operation.
- Cryptographic proof generation or verification.
- TEE/blockchain deployment.
- Claims of independent supervised-learning generalization from human-annotated labels.
- Re-identification or credential inference.

## Privacy and Ethics

The release contains no known private keys, tokens, encrypted payloads, proof bundles, TEE logs, blockchain state, or database dumps. Fields such as `content_driver_did`, `content_vehicle_did`, and `content_plate_hash` are semi-synthetic experiment fields, not real credentials, real license plates, or usable secrets.

## Known Limitations

- Several labels and predictor fields are generated within the same semi-synthetic experiment pipeline.
- Accuracy/Macro-F1 against rule-reference labels should be described as rule-reference agreement or policy-decision consistency, not independent external accuracy.
- Raw STATS19 source files are not included; users must obtain them separately if they need to rebuild the dataset from source.
- The license placeholder should be finalized before public release.
